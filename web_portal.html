Viceroyalty
noun · historical
The office, authority, or period of service of a viceroy; a territory ruled by a viceroy. In Indian history, British India was administered as a Viceroyalty under the Crown, with the Viceroy serving as the representative of the British Crown.

APPSCCE — Civil Services Examination
Group A — ACS / APS / APFS / APCC · 3-Stage Exam
▼
Prelims Mains (9 Papers) Interview
Prelims GS Paper I: History, Geography, Economy, Polity, Science, Environment, Current Affairs, AP-specific — 150 Qs / 300 Marks
Prelims CSAT Paper II: Comprehension, Logical Reasoning, Analytical Ability, Decision Making, Basic Numeracy — Qualifying (33%)
Mains Paper I: General Essay — 250 Marks
Mains Papers II–V: GS-I (History), GS-II (Governance), GS-III (Economy), GS-IV (Ethics) — 250 Marks each
Mains Papers VI–VII: Optional Subject Two Papers — 250 Marks each
Mains VIII–IX: General English & General Hindi — 300 Marks (Qualifying only)
Interview/Personality Test: 275 Marks — Critical analysis, AP social issues, leadership
